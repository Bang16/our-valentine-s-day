

# Valentine's Day Landing Page 💕

A minimal, romantic multi-screen experience with a soft, elegant aesthetic.

---

## Design & Aesthetic

- **Color palette**: Soft blush pink and warm beige tones
- **Typography**: Elegant serif font (like Playfair Display) for romantic headings
- **Layout**: Mobile-first, centered content
- **Animations**: Gentle fade-in transitions, subtle floating heart particles in the background

---

## Screen Flow

### Screen 1: Welcome
- Centered fade-in text: *"Happy Valentine's Day, Babe ❤️"*
- Soft, rounded "Continue" button below

### Screen 2: The Question
- Text fades in: *"Did you receive your package? 🎁"*
- Two elegant rounded buttons: **Yes** and **Not yet**

### Screen 3a: If "Not yet"
- Sweet message: *"Okay 🥹 Come back once you do… this moment is worth it."*
- Enhanced floating heart animation in background

### Screen 3b: If "Yes"
- Romantic reveal with emphasis and beautiful spacing:
  - *"Meet me at Paralia after work."*
  - *"I heart you more ❤️ Dress cute. I'll be smiling the whole time."*
- After a short delay, fade in: *"Thank you for being you."*

---

## Special Touches

- Floating heart particles throughout all screens
- Smooth fade transitions between each screen
- Warm, intimate feeling that matches the personal message
